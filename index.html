🖨️ Print & Play PDF (3×3, Letter)

Overlord export order: leader, then your 5 schemes, then your 50-card deck (in your reordered sequence). Double-sided cards print front then back. Scheme cards print at their native angle, same physical size as every other card — rotate the printed card by hand to read it.

🃏 playingcards.io CSV

Columns: name, front, back — one row per physical copy, in deck order. For an Overlord deck this downloads two files: the 50-card deck (leader first) and the 5-card scheme deck, separately.

📋 Plain-text decklist

Human-readable list you can paste into Discord/chat to share with friends.

📎 Copy to clipboard

Same plain-text decklist, copied instantly — no file, no timestamp change.

💾 JSON decklist

Exact, lossless — re-import this to restore the deck perfectly, including card order.
